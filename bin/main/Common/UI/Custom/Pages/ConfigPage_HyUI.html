Sobre o HorseFollow

This plugin creates a persistent bond between the player and a mount. Supports horses and rams. Binding is done using feed items (Horse Feed / Ram Feed). Once bound, the mount actively follows its owner. Follow distance and teleport behavior are configurable; automatic teleport can be fully disabled. The mount can be ordered to stay in place. You can call the bound mount using the Horn item or the /horsefollow call command. The goal is a simple, functional, and reliable taming system, without complex AI or unnecessary mechanics. Interaction is centered on items: feed to tame, horn to call. Commands are mainly used for configuration, status, and optional actions.
